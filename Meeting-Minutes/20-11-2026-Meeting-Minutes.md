# Meeting #9: International Data Spaces Association (IDSA) Task Force on Data Spaces and AI

**Date:** November 20, 2026 · **Time:** 11:02 – 11:15 CET · **Format:** Microsoft Teams (recorded)

---

## Meeting highlights

**New participants.** Hector Rico Lorenzo (NTT DATA), Keerthi Thomas and Robert Learney (Digital Catapult), Giuseppe Zangari (Aruba, Data Space Adoption Forum), Daniel Heider (Mobility Data Space) and Takuma Yagi (NTT DATA) joined the Task Force meeting for the first time and introduced themselves.

**Four pillars and questionnaire.** Anil Turkmayali presented the four pillars on the Miro board: agent governance and trust; AI-ready technology and architecture; use cases, value and outreach; testbed use cases and demos. The pillar questionnaire, sent to members by email on November 20, collects which pillars members want to work on, which tasks to start with, own task proposals with a definition of done and who wants to lead. The answers are used to define concrete tasks and assign responsibilities. Members can also keep adding ideas on the Miro board. Achim Meyer, who could not attend, offered via the chat to contribute to Pillars 1, 2 and 3, with Pillar 3 as his main focus.

**Merging Pillars 3 and 4.** Christoph Schlueter Langdon noted by email that Pillars 3 and 4 are closely intertwined (use cases and value versus testbed use cases and demos), while outreach could stand on its own and include standardization needs. The two pillars can be merged once the questionnaire has produced concrete tasks.

**Standardization.** Haluk Inanmis, who also takes part in the standardization working group, noted that the effect of the interplay between AI and data spaces on standardization is not yet covered by any pillar. Anil proposed a standardization strand and asked members to add standardization ideas in the questionnaire. Reinhold Achatz supported the topic but asked for precision: the Dataspace Protocol (DSP), the Decentralized Claims Protocol (DCP) and the overall architecture are already defined and should not be changed. Any new standardization work should build on top of them.

**AI testbed.** A group is already working on the AI testbed and documenting its mission statement. Task Force members who want to join are welcome to contact Anil.

**Horizon Europe call.** Anil shared the Horizon Europe topic [HORIZON-CL4-2027-04-DATA-03](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/opportunities/topic-details/HORIZON-CL4-2027-04-DATA-03), which fits the testbed and possible frameworks or tools of the Task Force. Japanese organizations can take part alongside European partners, and the call was also shared with Akira Sakaino. Submission is in March 2027. Digital Catapult, NEC, VTT, GMV and Athena Research Center expressed interest in a consortium. Robert Learney and Yasser Mohammad noted that preparation needs to start early.

---

## Decisions

- Concrete tasks, leads and responsibilities per pillar are defined from the questionnaire results.
- Pillars 3 and 4 can be merged once concrete tasks are identified.
- Standardization ideas are collected through the questionnaire. Any standardization work builds on top of the DSP, the DCP and the existing architecture without changing them.
- Consortium planning for HORIZON-CL4-2027-04-DATA-03 starts at the beginning of 2027 with the interested organizations.
