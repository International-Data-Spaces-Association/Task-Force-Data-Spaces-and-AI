# Meeting #8: International Data Spaces Association (IDSA) Task Force on Data Spaces and AI

**Date:** October 23, 2026 · **Time:** 11:03 – 11:16 CEST · **Format:** Microsoft Teams (recorded)

---

## Meeting highlights

**Member survey.** The survey is ready and member feedback has been incorporated. Distribution is waiting for internal IDSA processes and follows as soon as possible.

**Pillar descriptions and questionnaire.** Anil Turkmayali will share the expanded descriptions of the four pillars, including legal and organizational aspects, by email on Monday, October 26. A questionnaire of about 10 minutes goes out with them. It asks members to clarify the scope of each interest group and to propose tasks that count as outcomes, so that each group works toward tangible results.

**Eureka call.** Haluk Inanmis expressed interest in the Eureka Network (Globalstars) call shared on October 9, as Türkiye also takes part in it, but has no Japanese partner yet and asked for matchmaking. Yasser Mohammad responded from the Japanese side. Anil has also exchanged views with Akira Sakaino on the call and is collecting interest from parties in other countries. Interested organizations should contact Anil bilaterally by email.

**Testbed in Europe.** Several IDSA member organizations in Europe have expressed interest in the testbed development, and preliminary conversations are under way. The aim is to align first among European parties and then extend beyond Europe, building on the existing exchanges with Japanese and Chinese partners. Further organizations that want to contribute should contact Anil bilaterally.

**University of Tokyo and China.** Akira asked how to involve the University of Tokyo in the testbed. Anil confirmed it as the next step once a few more alignments within the group are done. Reinhold Achatz proposed inviting the University of Tokyo to the next testbed alignment call so it can see where things stand and step in when ready. Anil will send the invitation and arrange a second call if the time slot does not work for Japan. Polo from the China Future Internet Engineering Center (CFIEC) joined the meeting. He reported that researchers from the Chinese University of Hong Kong and other universities in Hong Kong and Macau are interested, and proposed inviting them to the next technical discussion. Anil will align with CFIEC.

**German delegation to Japan.** Akira noted that a German delegation, expected to include Lars Nagel, visits Japan in November, and that he joins on November 11. He suggested that IDSA present the Task Force's work at the meetings with Keidanren (Japan Business Federation), the Japanese government and the Robot Revolution & Industrial IoT Initiative (RRI), with an invitation to join the international testbed project. Anil agreed to prepare this, and Reinhold will share the idea as well.

**Access to materials.** Recordings are shared via Microsoft Teams and minutes are published in this repository. Access to the Miro board and to the Teams recordings now works for the participants who had reported problems.

---

## Decisions

- The expanded pillar descriptions and the pillar questionnaire go to members by email on October 26.
- The member survey is distributed once internal IDSA processes are complete.
- Interest in the Eureka call and in contributing to the testbed is collected bilaterally by email.
- The University of Tokyo is invited to the next testbed alignment call, with a separate call if the time slot does not suit Japan.
- The Task Force's work, including an invitation to join the international testbed, is prepared for the German delegation's visit to Japan in November.
