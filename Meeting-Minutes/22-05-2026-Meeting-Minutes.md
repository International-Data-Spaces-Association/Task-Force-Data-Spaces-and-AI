# Meeting #3 — IDSA Task Force on Data Spaces and AI

**Date:** 22 May 2026 · **Time:** 11:00 – 11:30 CET · **Format:** Microsoft Teams (recorded)

---

## Meeting highlights

**Positioning vs. the Telco Provider paper.** Anil opened the meeting with a coordination point: a parallel position paper is being prepared by the IDSA Telco Provider Community. Akira Sakaino confirmed that the Telco paper focuses on the training-data bottleneck and on telco-specific issues such as power shortages, traffic impact, trust and security. The Task Force paper focuses on trustworthy agentic participation in data spaces. The two papers are therefore complementary rather than competing. Arian Artman added that NTT Data has also started a paper on AI and data spaces; the group acknowledged the proliferation of efforts in this space and the value of seeking synergies. Anil will coordinate the positioning with the IDSA head office.

**Merging the working outline with Olaf's draft.** Olaf Gerd-Gemein had drafted a parallel version of the paper, with content generated as a directional contribution. The structure is largely identical to the working outline, but Olaf's draft adds significant material that fits under Chapters 4 and 5. Anil will merge both documents into a single source of truth and circulate the merged version by Tuesday 27 May.

**Section assignments.** The group walked through the table of contents and confirmed initial volunteers across chapters. Coverage is good for the conceptual sections in Chapters 1 and 2 and partial for the technical sections in Chapter 4. Volunteers include Arian Artman for the Introduction; Achim Meyer for several sections (the dual framing, separating training from use, Trust); Marko Turpeinen and Viivi Lähteenoja for how concepts match and the regulatory context; Gabriella Laatikainen for trusted AI services; Fernando López Aguilar for AI agent identities; Olaf Gerd Gemein and Peter Koen to be confirmed for key challenges and the AI–DS interface. The full assignments table will be published with the merged document on 27 May. Some sections (notably 4.3 and 4.6) will need additional contributors from the IDSA Working Group Architecture, which Anil will reach out to.

**Use cases / call for concrete examples.** Achim Meyer underlined that the use case chapter cannot rely on theory alone. Concrete examples where AI and data spaces have been implemented or at least conceptualized are essential to convince readers outside the IDSA community. Achim offered three projects from his own portfolio and asked all members to contribute their examples. Anil will reach out to the IDSA Working Groups to broaden the example base.

**Open question on terminology.** Achim raised whether the paper should explicitly distinguish AI from machine learning and data science. The question will be addressed during the merge.

**Onboarding additional contributors.** Arian Artman will share the paper with AI-experienced colleagues from NTT Data and onboard them to the meeting series and the GitHub repository.

---

## Decisions

- The Task Force paper and the Telco Provider Community paper will be positioned as complementary, with distinct focus areas (trustworthy agentic participation vs. training-data bottleneck and telco-specific issues)
- Anil to merge the current outline with Olaf's draft into one source of truth, circulated by Tuesday 27 May
- Initial section assignments confirmed; further contributors to be recruited via email and through the Working Group Architecture
